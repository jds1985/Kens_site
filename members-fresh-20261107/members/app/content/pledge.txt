Pledge of Word and Name
I, (give your name),
  give my word
  that I will not discuss the secrets of this Order,
  as such,
  with anyone except a brother or sister of the Order.
I acknowledge
  that a name or a word is a representative of its source.
I furthermore acknowledge
  that the giving of my word serves as a test,
  to myself and to others,
  of the value of my word.
