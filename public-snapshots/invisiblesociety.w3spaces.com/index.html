Ordo Therapeutae Mystae

Ancient Therapeutic Order of the Mystic Temple
 

There was a Jewish community in Alexandria, Egypt, called the Therapeutae, meaning "those who serve." "Therapeutae" has a double meaning, though.
1) In the sense of "healing," they professed alternative therapeutics better than that available in the cities.
2) In the sense of "worship," they professed to worship God as "The Being, who is better than The Good, purer than The One, and more archaic (ancient) than The Monad."
Today, the QaBbaListic Therapeutists™/© of the Invisible Society™/© practice a revived, but more advanced version of this profession.
